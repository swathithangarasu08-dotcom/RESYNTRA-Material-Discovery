# RESYNTRA

## AI-Based Automated Microstructural Analysis and Material Discovery from Agricultural Waste

> **From what the material looks like to what the material could become.**

---

## 🌱 Overview

**RESYNTRA** is an AI-assisted material discovery platform designed to explore the hidden material potential of agricultural waste through microstructural analysis.

Agricultural residues such as pomegranate peel, rice husk, sugarcane bagasse, coconut fibre, and other biomass materials contain complex microstructural characteristics that can influence their potential use as sustainable materials.

RESYNTRA creates a computational workflow that connects **microscopy, computer vision, material fingerprinting, AI-assisted analysis, application discovery, and 3D product visualization**.
